Technical innovations and an aerodynamic design elevate both the driving and the interior experience.

The front aero wing attaches the air to the bonnet, increasing aerodynamic efficiency and extending range.

The full-length panoramic roof lets more light into the cabin, creating a feeling of airy spaciousness.

The optional electrochromic film uses liquid crystals to actively reduce glare. The added double infrared-coating boosts climate control efficiency.
